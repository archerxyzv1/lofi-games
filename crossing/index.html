How to play

Ride the ice floes and boats across fifty frozen rivers to the far bank and its gold. Ships are hazards: never touch one. You have three lives.

  • ↑WHop forward, one step at a time
  • ←→ADStep sideways along the bank
  • ↓SStep back to safety
  • 🧊Ice floes and boats carry you with the current
  • 🚢Ships are hazards: cross the channel between them
  • 💧Open water with no floe underfoot costs a life
  • 🪙Gold coins are worth 25 points, on floes and the far bank
  • 🏝️Banks and the middle island are safe ground
  • PMPause · Sound on / off
  • TouchSwipe to hop, or use the arrow buttons
  • EnterStart · Cross again
