    How to play

    Slide down the three-lane ice track toward the moon at 3,000 m. Jump the ice blocks, logs, barriers, snowmen, snow rocks and sign boards, slide under the torii gates, switch lanes around the rocks. Chain coins to fill the Rush meter. Three hearts per run, ten stages.

    • ↑WSpaceJump over ice blocks, logs, barriers, snowmen, snow rocks and sign boards
    • ↓SSlide under torii gates
    • 🪙Coins in a row build your chain and Rush meter
    • ⚡Full meter: RUSH mode, fast and untouchable
    • ❤️🛡️⭐Pickups: a heart back, a shield, a Rush boost
