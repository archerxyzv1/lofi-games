How to play

Lofi bounces on his own. Steer him from platform to platform, ride the springs and rockets, and reach the moon 50,000 m up.

  • ←→ADSteer Lofi left and right
  • BounceLofi bounces by himself, just aim for the next platform
  • EdgesFly off one side and Lofi wraps around to the other
  • SpringLand on a spring and Lofi launches extra high
  • 🚀SpaceGrab a rocket, then Space or the Rocket button to ride it
  • 🌙The moon waits 50,000 m up, take all the time you need
  • IcePale cracked platforms break away, they give no bounce
  • FallingSlip below the bottom of the screen and the climb ends
  • PMPause · Sound on / off
  • TouchHold the steer arrows, tap Rocket to fly
  • EnterStart · Climb again
