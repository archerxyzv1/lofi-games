Help

Sign in to keep your XP, achievements and daily challenges together in one player account.

Find a game and its controls

The Games section on the home page lists all four games by name: Lofi Dash, Frozen Crossing, Lofi Rush and Lofi Climb. Each card shows that game's controls under its description, and each game keeps its own pause and sound controls on its game screen.

Tap Play on a card and the game opens right here in your browser, no downloads. The hub header stays at the top, so your profile and the way back are always one tap away.

Saved runs and local bests

Your best score in each game is saved on this device, so it is waiting for you when you come back in the same browser. Sign in and your bests sync to your player account too, so they follow you to your other devices.

A local best belongs to the browser it was set in. Clearing this site's browser data removes local bests and on-device saves, but anything already synced to your player account stays. Your data explains what is kept where.

Achievements, XP and daily challenges

There are 20 achievements, five in each game. Each game also awards XP for completing its 10 levels, so your total XP has no cap. Open Achievements to see every achievement, what it takes to earn it, and how close you are. When an achievement unlocks in a game, its XP is added to your player account.

Three daily challenges arrive every day and reset at 22:00 (10 PM) Asia/Dhaka time. Challenges count toward Challenge Days only; they award no XP. Finishing all three in one day banks a Challenge Day on your player page.

Your account

Profile & settings lets you edit your public username, pick an avatar, review the accounts linked to this player, and sign out. Sign in with Google, email, or a SUI Wallet, and use Link another account in Profile & settings to add a second sign-in method to the same player; your saved progress stays intact.

Signing out signs you out of Lofi Games only, never out of your Google account, and your synced progress is still here when you sign back in.

Report a problem

A useful report makes a problem much easier to find and fix. Include the name of the game, the device and browser you were using, the steps that led to the problem, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. A screenshot, or the score shown at the end of the run, helps a lot.

Keep personal sign-in details, passwords and wallet information out of any report you post in public.

Your data

What is kept on this device, what is kept in your player account, and the controls you have over both.

Playing on this device

Your browser keeps your local best score for each game, plus small bits of on-device state: a saved wallet address so a SUI Wallet can reconnect, your username-change count for the day, and whether you picked an avatar yourself.

These belong to this device and this browser. Clearing the site's browser data removes them and signs you out here; it does not erase the progress saved to your player account.

Your player account

A signed-in player has one shared player record, kept in Firebase: your public username, your chosen avatar, your XP, the achievements you have collected, your challenge days, and your synced best scores across the four games. That record is what lets the same progress follow you between devices.

Your player page shows your totals, and Achievements shows each achievement's state: collected, ready to claim, or in progress.

Sign-in and optional connections

Sign-in runs through Firebase Authentication, with the method you choose on the sign-in screen: Google, email and password, or a SUI Wallet. Linking another method in Profile & settings attaches it to the same player, so no second account is created.

A SUI Wallet connection is optional and separate: only your public wallet address is stored on your profile, and Lofi never asks for a seed phrase or a wallet password. Signing out ends your Lofi session on this site only; it does not sign you out of Google, and it does not delete saved scores or achievements.

Public names and reports

Your username, avatar and XP are public: they appear on the Leaderboard, which lists the top 25 players by XP. Pick a username you are happy for other players to see. Your email address and your sign-in details are never shown publicly.

If a problem in a game needs reporting, Help explains what to include so it can be found and fixed.

Questions and account controls

You can review your player, your linked accounts and your saved achievements from Your player and Profile & settings. There you can change your username, switch between your Google photo and the preset avatars, link or disconnect accounts, and sign out.

Help explains how the games, achievements and daily challenges work, and what to include when you report a problem.
