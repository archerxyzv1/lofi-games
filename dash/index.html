How to play

Jump over the road stickers, duck when you need to, and collect 🪙 coins. One hit ends the run. The world changes as you clear each level.

  • ↑WJump · press again in the air to dash
  • →DMove forward
  • ←AMove back
  • ↓ZDuck
  • SpaceJump · again in the air to dash
  • PMPause · Sound on / off
  • TouchTap to jump, tap again to dash, swipe down to duck
  • EnterStart · Run again
